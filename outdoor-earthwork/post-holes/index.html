Post Hole Calculator

Cylinder volume per hole: π × (d/2)² × depth × count, minus nothing (post displacement ignored = built-in buffer).

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

V = πr²h × count → bags = V ÷ yield, rounded up.

Worked example

Example: ten 12-in × 36-in holes: 10 × 2.36 cu ft ≈ 23.6 cu ft → ~40 eighty-lb bags; +5% → buy 42.

Questions

Should I subtract the post?

No — ignoring it adds a small safety buffer for spillage.
