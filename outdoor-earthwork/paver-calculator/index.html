Paver Calculator

Counts pavers from net area plus cuts, and sizes the gravel base and sand bedding from the same footprint.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Pavers = patio area ÷ paver face area, +10% cuts. Base gravel = area × base depth → yards.

Worked example

Example: 12×12 ft patio with 4×8-in pavers (0.22 sq ft each): 144 ÷ 0.22 ≈ 648 → buy ~713.

Questions

How much base under pavers?

Commonly 4–6 in compacted gravel + 1 in sand; follow manufacturer specs.
