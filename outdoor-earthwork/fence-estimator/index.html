Fence Calculator

Pickets = length ÷ (picket width + gap). Posts every 6–8 ft. Concrete ≈ 1 bag per post (editable by hole size).

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Pickets = ceil(L ÷ (w+gap)) + waste. Posts = ceil(L ÷ spacing) + 1.

Worked example

Example: 100-ft fence, 5.5-in pickets: 1200 ÷ 5.75 ≈ 209 pickets +10% → 230.

Questions

How deep are posts?

Commonly 1/3 of post in ground below frost line — check local code.
