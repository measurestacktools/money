Topsoil Calculator

Enter area and added depth to get bulk topsoil yards. Topsoil settles — add 10–20% for grading work.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Volume = area × depth → yards = m³ ÷ 0.7646, rounded up + settling allowance.

Worked example

Example: 200 sq ft × 6 in = 100 cu ft = 3.7 yd³; +15% settling → order 5 yards.

Questions

Will topsoil settle?

Yes — fresh topsoil can settle 10–20%. Order extra and re-grade.
