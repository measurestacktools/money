Mulch Calculator

Mulch depth is usually 2–4 inches. Get bulk yards plus bag count for top-ups.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Volume = area × depth. Yards = cu ft ÷ 27. Bags = cu ft ÷ editable bag size (default 2 cu ft).

Worked example

Example: 100 sq ft × 3 in = 25 cu ft = 0.93 yd³ → order 1 yard, or thirteen 2-cu-ft bags.

Questions

How deep should mulch be?

2–4 in (5–10 cm); keep it off stems and trunks.
