Board Foot Calculator

A board foot = 144 cubic inches. Enter thickness, width, length and piece count; add your price per board foot for cost.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

BF per piece = T(in) × W(in) × L(ft) ÷ 12. Total = BF × count. Cost = total × your price.

Worked example

Example: ten 2×4×8 ft boards: 2×4×8/12 = 5.33 BF each → 53.3 BF total.

Questions

Is a 2×4 really 2 by 4 inches?

No — nominal vs actual: a 2×4 is 1.5×3.5 in. Board feet use nominal sizes by convention; note this onitti your order.

What about metric?

Enter cm/mm and the tool converts to the board-foot equivalent.
