Stud Wall Calculator

Counts studs at on-center spacing plus plates (top + bottom, double top) for a framing shopping list.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Studs = floor(length ÷ spacing) + 1 end stud + corners/openings allowance. Plates = 3 × length (sole + double top).

Worked example

Example: 12-ft wall at 16 in OC: 12×12/16 = 9 + 1 = 10 studs + 1 extra → buy 11–12.

Questions

16 vs 24-in spacing?

16 in OC is standard for load-bearing; 24 in OC allowed for some non-bearing walls per code.
