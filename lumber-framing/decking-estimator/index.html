Decking Calculator

Boards run along the length; count = deck width ÷ (board width + gap). Screws ≈ 2 per joist crossing.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Boards = ceil(deckWidth ÷ (boardW + gap)). Fasteners ≈ boards × joists × 2.

Worked example

Example: 12×16-ft deck, 5.5-in boards: 144 ÷ 5.75 ≈ 26 boards 16 ft long.

Questions

What gap between boards?

About 1/8–1/4 in for drainage; wider for wet lumber that will shrink.
