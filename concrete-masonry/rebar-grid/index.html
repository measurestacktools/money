Rebar Calculator

Rebar forms a grid: bars each way = dimension ÷ spacing + 1. Weight uses standard bar weights (#3 = 0.376 lb/ft, #4 = 0.668 lb/ft).

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Bars_x = floor(L/spacing)+1; total length = bars_x×W + bars_y×L. Weight = length × lb/ft.

Worked example

Example: 20×20 ft slab at 18-in spacing → 14 bars each way → 560 ft of #4 ≈ 374 lb.

Questions

What size rebar for a slab?

commonly #3 or #4; spacing per plans/code. This tool estimates quantity only, not structural design.
