Concrete Slab Calculator

Enter your slab size to get concrete volume in cubic yards and an estimated number of premix bags. Bag yield is editable because actual yield varies by product — check the bag.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Volume = length × width × thickness (all converted to a consistent base unit internally, metres). Cubic yards = cubic metres ÷ 0.7646. Bags = volume ÷ editable yield per bag, rounded up, plus waste %.

Worked example

Example: 10 ft × 10 ft × 4 in = 33.3 cu ft (0.94 m³) → 1.23 yd³. At 0.60 cu ft per 80-lb bag: 33.3 ÷ 0.60 ≈ 55.6 bags; with 5% waste buy 59.

Questions

How thick should a slab be?

4 in (100 mm) is typical for patios and walkways; 5–6 in for driveways. This tool does not design structural slabs — follow local code.

Why is bag yield editable?

Because 80-lb bags vary by mix and manufacturer (commonly ~0.60 cu ft but not universal). Check your bag and adjust.

How much waste should I add?

5–10% is common for slabs to cover spillage and uneven subgrade.
