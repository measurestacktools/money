Mortar Calculator

Convert masonry units or a target volume into mortar bags. Yield per bag is editable because products differ.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

From units: mortar cu ft = units × editable cu ft per unit. Bags = cu ft ÷ editable yield, rounded up.

Worked example

Example: 100 blocks × 0.15 cu ft = 15 cu ft ÷ 0.60 = 25 eighty-lb bags.

Questions

Does mortar type matter?

Yes — Type N/S/M and pre-mixed vs site-mixed change yield. Check the bag.
