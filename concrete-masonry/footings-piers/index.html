Footing & Pier Calculator

Choose strip footings (rectangular trench) or round piers, enter dimensions and count, and get volume plus bag estimates.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Strip: V = L × W × D. Round pier: V = π × (d/2)² × depth × count. Convert to yards; bags = V ÷ yield.

Worked example

Example: trench 20 ft × 12 in × 12 in = 20 cu ft = 0.74 yd³ → ~34 eighty-lb bags at 0.60 cu ft each.

Questions

How deep should footings be?

Below local frost depth and per code — this tool estimates volume only, not structural design.
