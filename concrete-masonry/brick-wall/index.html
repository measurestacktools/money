Brick Calculator

Enter net wall area to get brick count plus mortar estimate. Bricks-per-sq-ft varies by brick size and joint — adjust the default.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Bricks = net area × editable bricks-per-sq-ft, rounded up + waste.

Worked example

Example: 100 sq ft × 6.5 = 650 bricks; +7% cuts → buy 696.

Questions

What about soldier courses and patterns?

Patterns change consumption — add extra waste (10–15%) for cuts and patterns.
