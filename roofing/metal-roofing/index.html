Metal Roof Calculator

Panels are sold by coverage width (default 36-in/0.91-m coverage, editable) and cut to length.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Panels = ceil(slopeWidth ÷ coverageWidth). Screws ≈ panels × length × 2 per foot + trim.

Worked example

Example: 30-ft eave ÷ 3-ft coverage = 10 panels per slope.

Questions

How much overlap?

Side lap per profile; end lap per manufacturer — add 5–10% trim waste.
