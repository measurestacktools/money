Gutter Calculator

Gutter = eave length + waste. Hangers every ~2 ft. Downspouts roughly every 30–40 ft per eave.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Gutter ft = eaves total × (1+waste). Downspouts = ceil(eave ÷ 35) per run (planning rule).

Worked example

Example: 60-ft eave → 63 ft gutter (+5%), 2 downspouts, ~30 hangers.

Questions

What size gutters?

5-in K-style suits most homes; 6-in for steep/large roofs — confirm with local practice.
