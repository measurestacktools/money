Roof Pitch Calculator

Pitch = rise per 12-in run. Angle = arctan(rise/run). Multiplier = √(1+(pitch/12)²).

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Angle° = atan(rise/run). Area multiplier = hypotenuse ÷ run.

Worked example

Example: 6/12 → 26.6° → multiplier 1.118.

Questions

Why does pitch matter for materials?

Steeper roofs have more true area than their footprint — multiply footprint by the multiplier.
