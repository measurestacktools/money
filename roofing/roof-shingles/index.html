Roof Shingle Calculator

A square = 100 sq ft. Bundles per square vary (default 3, editable). Pitch increases true area.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

TrueArea = footprint × pitchMultiplier. Squares = area ÷ 100. Bundles = squares × editable bundles/square.

Worked example

Example: 1,500 sq ft footprint at 6/12 (×1.118) = 1,677 → 16.8 squares → 53 bundles at 3/square +5% + starter/ridge.

Questions

What is a roofing square?

100 sq ft of roofed area — 17 squares ≈ 1,700 sq ft.

Do bundles per square vary?

Yes — typically 3–4 depending on shingle type. Check the wrapper.
