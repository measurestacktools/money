Drywall Calculator

Sheets from net area ÷ 32 sq ft; screws ≈ 32 per sheet; mud ≈ 1 gallon per 100 sq ft (editable).

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Sheets = ceil(netArea ÷ 32) + waste. Screws ≈ sheets × 32. Compound ≈ netArea ÷ 100 gal.

Worked example

Example: 12×12×8-ft room: walls 2×(12+12)×8 = 384 sq ft + ceiling 144 = 528 − 60 openings = 468 → 468 ÷ 32 = 14.6 sheets; +10% → buy 17.

Questions

4×8 or 4×12 sheets?

4×12 covers faster with fewer seams where it fits through doors and stairs.
