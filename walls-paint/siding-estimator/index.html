Siding Calculator

One square = 100 sq ft. Vinyl panels vary — count from area with 10–15% cut waste.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Squares = netArea ÷ 100. Panels ≈ squares × panels-per-square (product-specific; default 2 per sq for 12-ft D4).

Worked example

Example: 1,200 sq ft net = 12 squares; +10% → 13.2 → order 14 squares equivalent.

Questions

Should I include gables?

Yes — measure gable triangles (½×base×height) and add them.
