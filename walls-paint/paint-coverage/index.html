Paint Calculator

Coverage varies by paint and surface (default 350 sq ft/gal, editable). Subtract openings; add coats and waste.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Gallons = netArea × coats ÷ editable coverage, rounded up + waste.

Worked example

Example: 468 sq ft net × 2 coats ÷ 350 = 2.7 gal → buy 3.

Questions

Do I need primer?

On bare drywall, patches or dramatic color changes — roughly the same quantity as one coat.
