Laminate Floor Calculator

Box coverage varies (commonly ~20 sq ft) — enter your box coverage. Stagger waste ~10%.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Boxes = ceil(area ÷ editable box coverage × (1 + waste)).

Worked example

Example: 200 sq ft ÷ 20 sq ft/box = 10 +10% → 11 boxes.

Questions

Do I need underlayment?

Many laminates need it — order room area +10%; some vinyl has attached pad.
