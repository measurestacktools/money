Grout Calculator

Grout use depends on tile size, joint width and depth. Default 0.5 lb/sq ft is a planning figure — confirm on the bag chart.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Grout lb = area × editable lb per sq ft. Bags = lb ÷ bag size, rounded up.

Worked example

Example: 100 sq ft × 0.5 = 50 lb → two 25-lb bags.

Questions

Why is this only an estimate?

Wide joints and small tiles use much more grout; always check the coverage chart.
