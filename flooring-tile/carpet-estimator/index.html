Carpet Calculator

Carpet is sold by the square yard (9 sq ft). Rolls are 12 ft wide — seams planned separately.

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Sq yd = sq ft ÷ 9. Order +10% (rooms) to +15% (stairs/patterns).

Worked example

Example: 12×12-ft room = 144 sq ft = 16 sq yd → order ~18.

Questions

What about stairs?

Measure each tread+riser and add 15–20%; patterns need matching allowance.
