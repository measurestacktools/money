Tile Calculator

Tile count from area ÷ tile face area; grout in pounds is an editable assumption (default 0.5 lb/sq ft).

Enter your measurements and press Calculate.

Actual product yields vary — check the manufacturer's specs. Estimated cost (if shown) uses YOUR price, not a market price.

Formula

Tiles = area ÷ tileArea + waste (10% straight, 15% diagonal). Grout ≈ area × editable lb/sq ft.

Worked example

Example: 100 sq ft with 12×12-in tile: 100 tiles +10% → 110; grout ≈ 50 lb.

Questions

Straight vs diagonal layout?

Diagonal uses ~15% waste vs ~10% for grid, due to triangle cuts.
